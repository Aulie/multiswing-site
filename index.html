How it works

Set it up once, then stop touching your phone.

One device needs three steps. Two or three devices need a fourth at the front, to introduce them to each other. After that the app is watching for a strike and you are just hitting balls.

01Two or three devices

Connect

Open MultiSwing on each device, pick the same session, and give each one its job. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on — they talk straight to each other, with no network in between. On a single device there is nothing to connect, so this step doesn't appear.

02

Place

Every device about 3 m (10 ft) away, at hip height, a tripod under each. One behind the ball down the line, one square to your chest, and any replay screen where you can see it from address.

03

Frame

Fit your whole body and the full swing inside the frame. The banner turns green the moment the app can see you.

04

Swing

The replay opens by itself on the replay device, every time. Practice swings and waggles are ignored, so the library stays clean.

Detection

It waits for the strike, not for you to press record.

MultiSwing watches and listens at the same time — body motion through the camera, the crack of impact through the microphone. A swing is only logged when both agree, which is why rehearsals, waggles and the player in the next bay don't end up in your library.

Questions

Filming your own swing, answered.

Can I film my own golf swing without someone holding the phone?

That is the whole point of it. Put each device on a tripod about 3 m (10 ft) away at hip height and hit balls. Detection fires on the strike, so nobody presses record and nobody stands behind a camera instead of playing.

How do I record a golf swing from two angles at once?

One device behind the ball down the line, one in front of you square to your chest. They connect straight to each other and line their clips up on impact, so the replay shows both angles at the same instant of the swing.

Do I need two phones?

No. One device records and replays on its own, filming whichever angle you want. A second gives you either a dedicated replay screen or a second angle. A third gives you both angles plus a screen to watch them on.

Down the line or face on — which should I film?

It depends what you are chasing. Sway, spine tilt and hand position at the top read face on. Early extension and the path of your hands through the downswing need down the line. Film both and every check runs.

Does it need wifi, a signal or an account?

None of the three. The devices find each other directly over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth with no router in between, and the analysis runs on the device itself. Nothing is uploaded and there is nothing to sign in to.

What frame rate do you need to see a golf swing?

120 fps is enough to read the positions clearly. 240 fps, on devices that support it, still holds together when you slow the replay to a tenth of speed. Both sit in Settings, alongside 60.

Will it fill up with practice swings?

No. A swing is only logged when the camera and the microphone agree — body motion and the sound of the strike together. Rehearsals, waggles and the player in the next bay are ignored.

What does it need in order to see you?

Your whole body and the full swing inside the frame; the banner turns green the moment the app can see you. Good light and a background without people walking through it make detection more reliable.
